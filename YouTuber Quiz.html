The 3 Metrics That Matter

From Jimmy's 20,000+ hours studying virality

CTR

Click-Through Rate

Percentage of people who click your thumbnail after seeing it.

"I Spent 50 Hours in Ketchup" beats "I Spent 50 Hours in My Yard"

AVD

Average View Duration

How long people actually watch. The #1 most important metric.

AVP

Average View Percentage

What % of your video the audience watches on average.
